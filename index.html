03

Ventures

Things I founded and built from zero.

ShieldPay

Founder · Idea Stage · 2026

I started this after a month I was down to my last few dollars as a broke college student. I used what I had left on groceries at Walmart, and they accidentally charged me three times for a single item. Neither I nor my bank caught it. That's the gap ShieldPay exists to close: the duplicate charges and hidden fees that slip past both sides of the system, hitting hardest the people with the least room to absorb them.

Currently researching the detection model and talking to students living paycheck to paycheck about where they've been silently overcharged. Building the financial oversight institutions already have, for everyone else.
